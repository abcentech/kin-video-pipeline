**Editorial decisions to confirm**
- He opens straight into the reading of Joel 2:25 with no greeting first, so there is no "THE WORD" title: the scripture page is the opening. Because he starts reading in the very first second, the page appears about 2 s in, as he reaches "the years". The first words he says ("Joel 2:25. And I will restore to you") are on camera.
- He does **not** say the guide's declaration ("God saw every year. He gives me Himself."). He leads his own say-with-me line instead: "Say with me: God is my Father, and Jesus Christ is my life." The declaration card shows **his** line, timed to his words. The guide's wording is on the end card and in the description.
- He does **not** speak the response, and he does not pray the guide's prayers. The response (children and fathers) goes on a silent card in the natural 4-second pause after "receive His fatherhood through His Son". The prayer card shows his own words.
- The summary card "Today's three truths" sits in his natural 4.6-second pause after "…and let Him also restore you", at the end of truth 3.
- Only one short phrase is cut for being unclear: "Neither did you wipe us years. You know what He did?" in truth 2. The sentence before ("God did not send a new calendar") and the one after ("He gave us a life that locusts cannot eat") join cleanly.
- No retakes were found. The places the machine flagged as retakes are really lists ("How many years have you missed your son's birthday? … Christmases?", "For some of us it's months… years… decades", "Share this with your father / family / daughter / son"), so all of them are kept.
