**Editorial decisions to confirm**

- The scripture page cannot open on his very first word (nothing may start in the first 1.2 s of the body), so it opens as he reaches "that we are not consumed"; the first words ("It is of the LORD's mercies") are already lit when it appears.
- He never says the guide's declaration ("God keeps His promises. He sent His Son.") and never reads the response or the guide's prayers. So: the response is a silent card after "He won't go back on His word", before "Can we say prayer?"; the prayer plate carries his own words; the declaration is a silent card right after the prayer ("In Jesus Christ … we have prayed"), before "Did you receive it?". He does lead two say-it-with-me lines of his own ("God is faithful / My Father is faithful" and "God is my Father and Jesus Christ is my life"); they stay as he says them.

He never names **Truth 3** ("One promise, one night at a time"); its idea only comes in the prayer for fathers ("one promise kept at a time"). The Truth 3 chapter card is placed where he turns to broken promises at home ("So, don't be afraid to count on His promises, because somebody failed to keep their promise… the new shoe they didn't buy… the school fees").
