**Editorial decisions to confirm**
- He reads Galatians 4:6 first, before his greeting. The reading opens the body as the scripture page. There is no “THE WORD” chapter card, because the page is the chapter.
- He does not say the declaration as a say-after-me line. He does not speak the response or the guide's prayers either. So:
  - The response is a silent card after “…the word of God.”, just before “Can we pray?”.
  - The prayer plate carries his own words (four clear sentences).
  - The declaration (“God is my Father. I am His child.”) is a silent card right after the prayer's “Amen”, before “I'll see you again at the same time”.
- The film clip is the guide's whole range, trimmed to end at 28.7 s, after the boy's last line. It shows:
  - the coach kneeling to ask the boy his name;
  - “My joy has come”;
  - the children praying the Lord's Prayer (the clip's own “MATTHEW 6:9” title);

  It plays after the title. I checked the frames for scenes unsuitable for children and found none.
- There is no film cutaway inside the teaching. He never describes the film's story. The only shots in the clip without burned-in subtitles are under 2 s, apart from the coach kneeling, which he does not talk about.
- No name lower-third: the presenter's name was not supplied.
- The end card carries the safeguarding line. The day speaks of a father who left, one who “provoked” his child, and one who “was really abusive”.
- The “He is the Father of … every soul / every father / every grandchild” lines were flagged by the machine as retakes. They are not retakes: they are a deliberate list, and all of it is kept.
