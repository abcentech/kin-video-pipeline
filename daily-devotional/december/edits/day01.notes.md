## NOTES — for the producer

**Version 4 (25 Nov) — N2 grade.** What changed since version 3:
- **Colour.** The N2 grade, chosen by the producer, replaces look C. It is measured from this recording: the wall is balanced to neutral from its own paint, the yellow patches on the wall are calmed so they stop pulling the eye, his face is lifted to a fitted skin tone inside a tracked window, and blacks sit at one level. No tint, no scrim, no vignette. N2's own check of the graded master is in `december/work/day01/logs/n2-measure.log`.
- **Joins.** Every shortened pause was tested on the real frames either side of it. Where his head had moved, a short pause is now kept whole (25 of them) and a long one became a clean cut with a reframe (14). The 15 dissolves that remain are ones where the two frames match, so the faint double image of version 3 is gone. The episode is about 3 seconds longer for it.
- **Nothing laid over the picture.** Earlier versions still carried a soft dark vignette in the corners, a dark wash behind the subscribe plate, and a dark floor across the lower 40 % of the frame under the prayer words; all darkened the recorded picture and are removed. The rendered picture now measures the same as the graded footage. In their place: the logo sits on a small dark plate (like the day badge), the prayer words ride their own plate (like the captions), and the side text has a tighter dark edge.
- **Render.** Finals are rendered from lossless frames, so the orange shift of earlier versions cannot happen; a frame of each finished file is still measured against the master.
- **Background and film** as in version 3: the recorded wall is never replaced, darkened or faded; the motion-picture EIGHT O'CLOCK clip plays seconds 1.0–49.8, after its advisory card.
