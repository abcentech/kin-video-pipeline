# THE FATHER SHOWED UP — the pass/fail sheet

Nothing ships until every line passes on the FINAL files (not on stills alone). One polish round after
the first full pass; what is still open after that goes on the day's `NOTES` list in `DayNN_QC.md`
and is said plainly to the producer.

## The cut
1. No retake, false start, slate chatter or off-mic word survives; no word is clipped at any join.
2. No pause longer than the edit's `maxPause` + 0.1 s; no join lands inside a breath that reads as a gasp.
3. Every hard cut changes the framing (no visible jump cut); every soft join is invisible at normal speed.
4. The first spoken word arrives within 1 s of frame 0 (cold open) — no logo, no fade-in first.

## The words
5. Captions match what he says, proof-read against the Teacher's Edition; scripture is KJV, letter for letter.
6. No caption appears more than 0.25 s before its word, none overlaps a card, none leaves the band.
7. Every scripture he quotes has a page or a card; the day's main text is a page with the phrase the
   teaching hangs on emphasised.

## The picture
8. His face is the brightest thing in frame; skin natural; no colour cast on the wall; no visible vignette edge.
9. Every takeover arrives through the door of light; no cue is static for more than 12 frames.
10. Nothing covers his eyes or mouth; nothing sits outside title-safe; type never smaller than the minimums.
11. Each of the three truths has its chapter and at least one point or illustration; something changes
    at least every 18 s.
12. Illustrations are in the one series style, the story happens in the frame, and they breathe.
13. Brand: on-file logo top-right, day dial, wordmark gauge; real YouTube mark; never a drawn logo, never emoji.
14. Subscribe nudge once at 30–45%, share nudge once at 65–80%, neither over scripture, prayer or declaration.
15. The episode closes: recap → declaration → tomorrow's card with the prayer-service ribbon → sign-off.

## The sound
16. Masters −14 ±0.7 LUFS, true peak ≤ −1.0 dB; voice never masked (bed ≥ 18 dB under while he speaks).
17. No click at any join; no SFX on top of a syllable; the theme is heard in full on the title and the end card.

## The trailer and shorts
18. Trailer 35–50 s, hooks in the first 3 s, teases without teaching, ends on the open loop + the day card.
19. Each short is one complete thought, hook title on screen at frame 0, readable with the sound off,
    nothing important in the platform UI zones, ends on the YouTube card.
20. 16:9 and 9:16 both reviewed as stills AND as frames pulled from the final mp4.

## The files
21. Durations match the plan; no black/white/frozen runs; A/V in sync (±2 frames).
22. SRT/VTT/transcript/socials/thumbnail present; file names follow PIPELINE.md.
